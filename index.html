Getting here

Easy to find, even on your first trip to Goa

By air

Dabolim (GOI) is about 45 minutes. Mopa (GOX) is about 70 minutes. Share your flight number in the notes and we can arrange a driver.

By train

Thivim and Madgaon are the usual stations. Taxis and prepaid cabs run to Calangute through the day.

Address

Stay info

What you need at check-in

Photo ID

Every guest over 18 must show original photo ID. Indian guests: Aadhaar, passport, driving licence or voter ID. Foreign guests: passport with a valid visa or e-visa.

Foreign travelers

We complete the statutory C-Form from your passport. Keep the same passport you enter in this form. A printed or digital visa copy helps the desk move faster.

Age & children

The primary guest must be 18+. Children sharing a bed with parents are welcome; request a crib or extra bed while booking if you need one.

Payment

We charge in Indian Rupees. International cards are accepted. Your room is held for 15 minutes at review so nobody else can take it while you pay.
